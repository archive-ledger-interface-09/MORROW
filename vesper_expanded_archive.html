Executive summary / read before contact

A-017 is a sentient, humanoid-presenting anomalous organism exhibiting unstable morphology, nonlocal feeding behavior, and a pronounced disregard for human bodily autonomy. The subject is neither consistently hostile nor reliably benevolent. Recorded interactions indicate that she understands injury, fear, and death, and can exploit that understanding for amusement, coercion, or convenience.

Attempts at prolonged isolation correlate with increased environmental distortion and destructive agitation. As a result, Vesper has authorized limited movement under a dedicated escort model. The assigned bodyguard serves primarily as an intervention and supervision officer; protection from outside threats is a secondary but necessary responsibility.

“You keep calling it an incident. I keep telling you it was funny.” — A-017, post-event interview

Known preferences

Sweet processed foods, public attention, novelty, soft furnishings, objects that belong to others, and being allowed to choose her own route through a building.

Known triggers

Physical confinement, boredom, public humiliation, perceived betrayal, involuntary sedation, and unauthorized contact with belongings she considers hers.

OBS-014 / Reward association

When rewarded for compliant behavior, A-017 demanded progressively larger compensation. Refusal produced a verbal threat, followed by immediate attempts to negotiate. Investigators concluded that reward systems are useful only when boundaries remain consistent.

MEMO / 04-118 — The question of intent

Our repeated error is treating the subject's capacity for social engagement as proof of human moral reasoning. She knows when an action is forbidden. She understands why we forbid it. This has never meant that she agrees with the prohibition.

MEMO / 04-129 — The escort paradox

Isolation creates measurable destabilization, but social access exposes staff and civilians to avoidable risk. The escort program is therefore a compromise, not a declaration of safety. It is entirely possible that A-017 has learned to perform compliance specifically to preserve access to the outside world.
